## Introduction

The SEO world is living through the biggest transformation in its history since search engines were born. The rise of large language models such as GPT-4o, Gemini, and Claude, together with AI-based search features like Google AI Overviews and answering engines like Perplexity, has forever changed how users search for information.

Today, [AI and SEO services](/services/ai/) are no longer a luxury option; they are the backbone of every modern digital-growth strategy. In this guide, we review the Asr SEO team's practical, battle-tested techniques for exploiting AI tools across all dimensions of on-page, technical, and content-strategy SEO.

## Traditional SEO and its limits

In the traditional SEO approach, teams spent weeks on:

- Manually categorizing spreadsheets with thousands of keywords
- Hand-writing page meta descriptions and titles with repeated human errors
- Time-consuming analysis of server logs and structural site issues
- Creating content with no modeling of competitor content depth

By contrast, smart use of machine-learning algorithms lets SEO specialists automate repetitive work and focus on big-picture strategy and creating real value for the audience.

## Practical steps for running SEO with AI

### 1. Keyword research and semantic clustering

With language models, you can organize keyword phrases into semantic topic clusters based not on surface letter similarity but on hidden search intent.

```
AI SEO topic cluster:
├── Pillar page: AI and SEO services
├── Cluster topics:
│   ├── Content-production automation and the content lifecycle
│   ├── Data analysis and search-trend forecasting

│   └── Code optimization and technical playbooks
```

### 2. E-E-A-T-based content briefs

Before writing any article, AI can scan Google's top 10 results and pinpoint content gaps:
- Questions users asked in forums that competitors never answered
- New statistics not yet reflected in old articles
- Tables and calculation formulas that make the topic easier for readers to grasp

### 3. Optimizing front-end code and schema markup (Schema.org)

AI multiplies the speed of implementing structured data several times over. With coding algorithms, precise schema code — `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `Article` — can be generated for every page so search engines understand each page's nature without ambiguity.

## Google's official view on AI content and E-E-A-T

One of clients' most common questions is: **Does Google penalize sites that use AI?**

Google's official answer is fully transparent: Google does not punish how content is produced; it judges its quality and value. In its helpful-content-system updates, Google emphasizes the E-E-A-T framework:

| E-E-A-T criterion | Human role | AI role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Experience** | Presenting real case studies, screenshots, and lived experience | Collecting data and documenting test procedures |
| **Expertise** | Specialist review and correcting technical and calculation errors | Suggesting logical structure and comprehensive outlines |
| **Authoritativeness** | Earning authoritative backlinks and collaborating with industry voices | Identifying reference sources and reputable sites to cite |
| **Trustworthiness** | Transparency in introducing authors, company address, and policies | Aligning article formats with fact-checking protocols |

## How do we prepare for Search Generative Experience and AI engines?

For your website to be introduced and cited as a trusted source by Google's AI and chatbots like ChatGPT and Claude:
1. **Direct answers in the first paragraph (AEO)**: at the start of each section, give the question's precise answer in 2 to 3 clear sentences.
2. **Use an llms.txt file**: place structured documentation at the website root so AI web crawlers recognize your business structure.
3. **Provide comparison tables and numeric data**: language models especially love figures, percentages, and summary tables, and place them faster as quotes in their output.

## September 2026 update: the era of AI Overviews and the zero-click economy

This article was first published in November 2024. Since then, the predictions about Search Generative Experience have become reality — no longer talk of "possibility" but of measured numbers. Below, read the key 2026 developments.

### AI Overviews went from a test to the mainstream

BrightEdge data shows AI Overviews appearing on roughly **48% of tracked Google queries**, up from about 30% a year earlier (BrightEdge, 2026: https://web.archive.org/web/20260805203541/https://www.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights/ai-overviews-one-year-presence-size-citing). Growth from a fringe feature to half the results page means no SEO strategy can ignore it.

### Click-through rates fell — but SEO is not over

The most serious research here comes from Seer Interactive; its 2026 edition covers 53 brands, 5.47 million queries, and 2.43 billion impressions (Seer Interactive, April 2026: https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update), and the table figures below come from the September 2025 update of the same study on informational queries (Seer Interactive, 2025: https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update). The headline results:

| Metric (September 2025) | Without AI Overview | With AI Overview | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic CTR | 1.62% | 0.61% | 61% drop on queries with AIO |
| Paid CTR | 13.04% | 6.34% | 68% drop on queries with AIO |

Ahrefs, on 300,000 keywords, likewise showed the position-one page's CTR falling about **58%** in the presence of AI Overviews; the previous edition of the same study in April 2025 had reported a **34.5%** drop — meaning AI Overviews' effect worsened through 2025 (Ahrefs, February 2026: https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/).

### The winners: pages that get cited

The key point is that an AI Overview's presence does not mean traffic death — it means its destination changes. Per the same data:

- Pages **cited inside the AI Overview** receive about **120% more clicks** per impression than uncited pages on the same query (Seer Interactive, 2026: https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update).
- These pages' impressions **more than doubled** in October and November 2025, while clicks stayed nearly flat (Seer Interactive, 2026: https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update).
- Users arriving via the AI Overview path are more targeted and convert at better rates.

Simply put: **being cited in the AI answer has become more valuable than ranking first in traditional results.**

### Which queries are most vulnerable?

| Query type | AI Overview appearance rate | Impact severity |
|---|---|---|
| Comparative ("X vs. Y") | 95.4% | Very severe |
| Question-based (what / why / how) | 85.9% | Very severe |
| Local ("near me") | 76.9% | Severe |
| General informational | 36% | High |
| Commercial / research | 8% | Low |
| Transactional (purchase) | 5% | Low |

Practical takeaway: generic definitional and educational content suffered the most damage, but service and product (transactional) pages still largely operate on traditional SEO logic. Table source: Seer Interactive, April 2026 — https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update

### Five actions to take in 2026

1. **Measure exposure (AIO Exposure Audit)**: in Search Console, review your key clusters split by informational vs. transactional. An impression spike paired with CTR collapse means an AI Overview is active on that query.
2. **Produce first-hand data**: proprietary research, internal statistics, case studies, and real project outcomes are what a language model cannot simulate from elsewhere.
3. **Extractable structure**: a definition at the start of each section, comparison tables, numbered lists, and direct answers in 2 to 3 sentences.
4. **Continuous updates**: content freshness is a competitive advantage in the AI-answer world, not a formality.
5. **Change the success metric**: measure "clicks per impression" and "citation counts in answers" instead of rank.
