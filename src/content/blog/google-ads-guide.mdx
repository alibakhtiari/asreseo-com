## Intro to Google Ads
Google Ads is the world's largest online advertising platform, showing your ads at the exact moment users search.

### Google Ads Benefits

1. **Instant results**: Unlike SEO, which takes months
2. **Precise targeting**: By location, time, device, and behavior
3. **Precise measurement**: Full tracking of ROI and performance
4. **Flexibility**: Continuous tweaking and optimization

### Key Google Ads Stats

- **3.5 billion** daily searches on Google
- **Ad clicks**: Users with purchase intent click paid results too
- **Average $2** return for every $1 invested

## Keyword Research and Selection
Keywords are the heart of search campaigns.

### Keyword Research Steps

1. **Identify core terms**
   ```
   Main product: running shoes
   ↓
   Core terms: running shoes, sneakers, walking shoes
   ```

2. **Use the Keyword Planner tool**
   - Monthly search volume
   - Suggested bid
   - Competition level

3. **Analyze competitor keywords**
   - Review competitor ads
   - Spot new opportunities

### Keyword Match Types

#### Broad Match
```
Keyword: running shoes
Shows for: shoes, sporty, sport sneakers, buy shoes
```

#### Phrase Match
```
Keyword: "running shoes"
Shows for: buy running shoes, men's running shoes
```

#### Exact Match
```
Keyword: [running shoes]
Shows for: only running shoes
```

### Keyword Selection Strategy

1. **A varied mix**: Combine broad, phrase, and exact instead of relying on one type
2. **Negative keywords**: Filter out irrelevant traffic
3. **Brand keywords**: Protect your brand

## Writing Compelling Ads
A good ad can noticeably lift CTR.

### Text Ad Structure

```
Headline 1: Buy running shoes with a special discount
Headline 2: Free local delivery
Headline 3: Authenticity guarantee
Description: Top running shoe brands at fair prices...
```

### Principles of Effective Ad Copy

1. **Use the keyword in the headline**
2. **Offer a unique benefit**
3. **A clear call to action (CTA)**
4. **Create urgency**

### Optimized Ad Example

```
Headline: Men's Running Shoes | Special Discount
Description: ✓ Free delivery ✓ Authenticity guarantee ✓ Cash on delivery
CTA: Shop now!
```

### Key Tips

- **A/B testing**: Test at least 3 ad versions
- **Use extensions**: Extra links, calls, locations
- **Mobile-friendly**: Optimize for mobile

## Landing Page Optimization
A good landing page can noticeably lift conversion rates.

### Traits of an Effective Landing Page

1. **Message match with the ad**
   - Same message
   - Similar design
   - Same CTA

2. **Load speed**
   - Under 3 seconds
   - Image optimization
   - Using a CDN

3. **Clear design**
   - Prominent CTA
   - Minimal distractions
   - Simple structure

### Essential Landing Page Elements

```html
Strong headline
↓
Explanatory subheadline
↓
Key benefits (3-5 items)
↓
Product images/video
↓
Prominent CTA button
↓
Customer testimonials
↓
Contact and support
```

### Optimization Tips

- **Mobile-first**: A large share of clicks comes from mobile
- **Simple forms**: Only the minimum required fields
- **Trust building**: Security badges, certifications

## Budget Management and Bidding
Proper budget management guarantees long-term success.

### Bidding Strategies

#### 1. Manual CPC
- Full price control
- Good for beginners
- Needs constant monitoring

#### 2. Target CPA
- Targets cost per conversion
- Good for sales
- Needs historical data

#### 3. Target ROAS
- Targets return on investment
- Good for e-commerce
- The most advanced method

### Budget Management Tips

1. **Start with a limited budget**
   ```
   Days 1-7: baseline test budget
   Days 8-30: scaled budget based on performance
   Later months: based on performance
   ```

2. **Split budget by performance**
   - Most of the budget: top-performing keywords
   - Part of the budget: new keywords
   - A small part: testing and experiments

3. **Daily monitoring**
   - Check budget spend
   - Adjust bids
   - Spot opportunities

### Key KPIs

- **CTR**: Above the industry average
- **Quality Score**: Above 7
- **CPC**: Below the projected budget
- **Conversion rate**: Per industry benchmarks

## September 2026 Update: Key Google Ads Changes

This guide was written in January 2024. Its principles (keyword research, ad structure, landing pages, budget management) still hold, but five changes to the platform itself have landed — ignore them and a large share of your budget goes to waste.

### 1. AI Max for Search Campaigns

The biggest 2026 change in search is **AI Max**. It isn't a new campaign type but a set of capabilities enabled on existing search campaigns:

- **Broader matching**: To expand reach, Google combines Broad Match keywords, asset relevance, and landing-page content understanding — not just traditional keyword logic.
- **Ad-text personalization**: More relevant messaging per query, built from your existing copy, assets, landing-page content, and generative AI.
- **Final URL expansion**: Google can send traffic to the most relevant page on your site, not necessarily the URL you set.
- **Governance controls**: Brand settings, allowed and excluded URL lists, and location-of-interest controls.

**Practical warning**: AI Max suits accounts with accurate conversion tracking, quality landing pages, and enough conversion volume. Enabling it blindly on an account with weak signals is costly. Broad matching must be paired with strong Smart Bidding, negative keywords, and suitable destination pages.

### 2. Call-Only Ads Are Going Away — You Have a Deadline

This change has a fixed timeline, and ignoring it means losing phone leads:

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| **February 2026** | Creating new call-only ads is no longer possible |
| **February 2027** | Existing call-only ads stop serving |

**Required action**: Google's replacement path is **responsive search ads (RSAs) with a call asset**. If a big share of your leads comes by phone, treat 2026 as your migration year and don't wait for the final months. When migrating, configure call conversion tracking correctly.

### 3. Performance Max Got More Transparent

Performance Max isn't obsolete, but its biggest flaw — poor visibility — has been partly fixed. In 2026 its reporting includes **channel-level reports** and **asset-level segmentation** (by device, time, conversion, and network). If you previously avoided PMax over reporting opacity, now is the time to retest it with a controlled budget.

### 4. Ads Now Appear in AI Overviews

Ads can now show **above, below, and in some cases inside** Google's AI answers. Search, Shopping, and Performance Max campaigns are eligible. Two key points:

- **No exclusive targeting**: You can't choose to show ads only in AI Overviews.
- **No dedicated opt-out either**: These impressions are part of the auction environment.

Impression volume depends on geography, language, campaign type, user intent, and ad relevance.

### 5. Asset Quality Went From "Good Advice" to "Operating Infrastructure"

Because AI-powered outputs feed directly on your assets and landing-page content, weak input produces weak output. In 2026, apply these to every asset:

- A clear, differentiating value proposition
- Strong promotional language
- Concrete proof points (numbers, certifications, portfolio)
- Varied messaging matched to different user intents
- Full alignment with the landing page

This principle covers more than copy — headlines, descriptions, product feeds, images, and videos alike.

### Recap: Account Update Checklist

1. Audit conversion settings; make sure primary conversions are genuinely valuable and offline results flow back into the platform.
2. Migrate call-only ads to RSAs with a call asset.
3. Evaluate AI Max on one test campaign, not the whole account.
4. Review assets and landing pages before enabling any AI-powered feature.
5. Separate low-value from high-value conversions in reporting.
