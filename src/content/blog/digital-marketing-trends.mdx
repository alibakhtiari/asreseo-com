## AI in Marketing
2026 continues the turning point that began in 2023 for AI in marketing — except we've now moved past the experimentation phase.

### AI Applications in Marketing

#### 1. Content Production
```
Applications:
├── Writing blog articles
├── Producing ad copy
├── Creating images and video
└── Automatic content translation
```

**Benefits:**
- **High speed**: Content produced in a fraction of the time
- **Low cost**: Dramatically lower content production costs
- **Consistency**: Maintaining brand tone and style

#### 2. Data Analysis and Prediction

**Predictive Analytics:**
- Predicting customer behavior
- Identifying the best send times
- Estimating purchase likelihood

**Practical example:**
```python
# Analyzing customer purchase probability
customer_score = ai_model.predict([
    'age': 35,
    'previous_purchases': 3,
    'time_on_site': 450,
    'email_opens': 12
])
# Result: 85% purchase probability
```

#### 3. Chatbots and Support

**Features of today's chatbots:**
- Natural language understanding
- Answering complex questions
- Smart handoff to humans
- Learning from interactions

### Popular AI Tools

1. **ChatGPT & GPT-4**: Content production and copywriting
2. **Midjourney**: Image generation
3. **HubSpot AI**: Marketing automation
4. **Jasper**: Marketing content production

### Tips for Using AI

- **Combine with human creativity**: AI is a tool, not a replacement
- **Quality control**: Always review AI output
- **Personalization**: Tune AI to your brand and audience

## Personalized Marketing
Personalization has gone from nice-to-have to necessity.

### Personalization Levels

#### Level 1: Basic Personalization
```
- Using the customer's name

- General suggestions based on past purchases
```

#### Level 2: Intermediate Personalization
```
- Content based on website behavior
- Segmented emails
- Dynamic pricing
```

#### Level 3: Advanced Personalization
```
- A unique experience for every user
- Predicting future needs
```

## September 2026 Update: Which Trends Survived?

This article was first published in January 2024. Now, more than two years later, it's time to look back at which predictions came true, which were exaggerated, and what should be added to the list.

### What We Predicted and Got Right

- **AI at the center of everything**: This trend didn't just materialize — it graduated from "helper tool" to "operating infrastructure." The key difference in 2026 versus 2024 is that the conversation has moved from "using AI" to "AI agents": systems that carry out parts of the work without moment-to-moment supervision.
- **Personalization**: Still the top priority, but the way it's powered has changed. With third-party cookies restricted, the pillar of personalization has shifted from purchased data to **first-party data**.
- **Video content**: Video's dominance was confirmed — except the focus moved from long-form video to short vertical video on social platforms.

### What Needs Adjusting

- **Augmented and virtual reality (AR/VR)**: This trend moved slower than expected. Mass adoption never arrived, and today it's mostly a complementary shopping feature (like product previews) rather than a standalone marketing channel. Heavy investment in it isn't economically justified yet.
- **Sustainability and social responsibility**: Its importance holds, but broad, undocumented claims in this area now carry serious reputational risk. Sustainability messaging must come with numbers, reports, and verifiable evidence.

### Three New Trends Missing From the Original

1. **Zero-click search**: A large share of searches now get answered right on the results page and never become clicks. The direct consequence: your success metric must shift from "visit counts" to "brand visibility and share of answers."
2. **Search on social networks**: A significant share of users, especially younger generations, start their searches on social platforms instead of search engines. That means your content must be present and discoverable outside your website too.
3. **Optimization for answer engines (AEO/GEO)**: The goal of content production is no longer just ranking in the results list — it's **being cited in AI-generated answers**. For the details of this approach, read our [AI and SEO](/blog/ai-seo-guide/) article.

### Practical Takeaway for Local Businesses

Instead of chasing every trend, invest in three pillars: your customers' first-party data, short localized video content, and a content structure that answer engines can extract. To prioritize for your budget and market, get a [free consultation](/consultation/).
