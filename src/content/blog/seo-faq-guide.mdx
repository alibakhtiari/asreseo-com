<h2 id="cluster-basics">1. General questions and SEO fundamentals</h2>

### What is SEO, and how does it differ from Google Ads?
SEO is the process of organically optimizing a website to earn top rankings in search engines without paying per click. Google Ads, by contrast, appears instantly at the top of results once funded but stops the moment the credit runs out. SEO is a long-term, compounding asset.

### Do we still need SEO, or is social media enough?
Yes, social media is built on triggering emotion and entertainment, while the Google audience searches with a clear intent to buy, solve a problem, or compare products. Organic-search conversion rates are usually higher than social media's.

### What is the difference between white-hat, black-hat, and gray-hat SEO?
White-hat SEO fully complies with Google's webmaster rules and guidelines. Black-hat SEO uses deceptive techniques, cloaking, and spam that ultimately lead to domain penalties. Gray-hat SEO covers techniques that circumvent Google's rules but are executed skillfully enough that Google's systems treat them as natural.

### Why does my site's Google ranking keep going up and down?
Ranking fluctuation (Google Dance) is completely natural in SEO. It happens because of periodic algorithm tests, competitor activity, content updates, and reviews of user-interaction signals. Rankings stabilize after a few weeks.

### What is the difference between local SEO and nationwide SEO?
Local SEO attracts geographically specific customers through Google Maps business listings and location-based queries, while nationwide SEO is designed to compete country-wide with no geographic limits.

### What is semantic SEO?
Semantic SEO optimizes content around entities, topical concepts, and word relationships instead of mechanically repeating a single keyword.

<h2 id="cluster-technical">2. Technical and specialist technical-SEO questions</h2>

### What are Core Web Vitals, and why does Google care about them?
The vital web metrics are LCP (how fast the largest visual element loads), INP (how fast the page responds to user interactions), and CLS (visual stability — elements not jumping around), which evaluate real site performance from the user's perspective.

### Why don't my site's pages get indexed by Google?
The main causes of non-indexing: an accidental noindex tag, a blocked path in robots.txt, server errors (5xx), a very slow host, or content Google's helpful-content algorithm deems low quality.

### What is a sitemap (Sitemap.xml), and what is it for?
An XML file listing all valid pages and their last-update dates, which helps Google's crawlers discover pages faster and understand the site's structure.

### What is the canonical tag, and when is it used?
The canonical tag tells Google which version is the original among several similar or overlapping versions of a page (such as shop filters or with/without-slash variants), preventing duplicate-content errors.

### How does schema (Schema Markup / JSON-LD) affect SEO?
Structured-data schema encodes what your content means for search engines and lets you earn rich snippets — rating stars, FAQs, and prices — in search results.

### What is the difference between a 301 and a 302 redirect?
A 301 redirect means a permanent page move and transfers all SEO equity (link equity) to the destination URL, while a 302 is temporary and transfers no equity.

<h2 id="cluster-content">3. On-page SEO and content-strategy questions</h2>

### What is the ideal article length for SEO?
Google does not rank by word count; depth of answer coverage and satisfying user need are what count. For tutorial articles, 1,500 to 2,500 words usually provides the depth needed to cover a topic fully.

### What is keyword cannibalization, and how is it fixed?
Cannibalization happens when two or more pages on your site compete over the same keyword. The fix is merging weaker pages into the stronger one with a 301 redirect, or changing each page's keyword target.

### What is search intent?
It is the user's intent behind the search, divided into 4 broad types: informational, navigational, commercial, and transactional.

### How does the topic-cluster structure work?
One comprehensive pillar page is created covering the broad topic. Then more specialized articles (cluster content) expand on sub-aspects and connect to the pillar page via internal links.

### Does keyword-density repetition still matter?
No, the keyword-density era is over. Today Google understands synonyms and related phrases (LSI), and over-repetition triggers a keyword-stuffing penalty.

### Why do a site's old articles lose their rankings over time?
Over time, information goes stale, competitors publish newer content, and Google's freshness signal weakens. The fix is periodically updating articles with fresh information.

<h2 id="cluster-authority">4. Authority, link-building, and off-page SEO questions</h2>

### What is Page Authority, and how does it differ from Domain Authority?
Page Authority (PA) is a logarithmic score from 1 to 100 measuring the ranking potential of one specific page, while Domain Authority measures the strength of the whole domain.

### What is the difference between follow and nofollow links?
Follow links tell search engines to pass SEO value (link juice) to the destination page. Nofollow links tell crawlers not to follow the link, although Google now treats them as a hint (an auxiliary signal).

### What is an advertorial, and what are its principles?
It is a promotional yet value-adding article published in reputable, high-traffic news media, containing a few follow links to your website to raise domain authority.

### What is anchor text, and how should it be distributed?
It is the text a link sits on. The anchor-text profile should look natural and mix brand names, exact-match keywords, combined phrases, and generic links (such as "click here").

### What are the dangers of low-quality or spam backlinks?
Buying bulk, cheap backlinks from link farms (weak PBNs) gets flagged by Google's Penguin algorithm and causes severe ranking drops or even full removal of the domain from the index.

### How can toxic backlinks be cleaned up?
First, toxic links are identified with tools like Ahrefs or Semrush. Then Google's crawlers are told to ignore them through the official Disavow Links tool in Search Console.

<h2 id="cluster-budget">5. Budget, timeline, and SEO-agency questions</h2>

### How long does SEO take to become profitable?
SEO is a medium- to long-term investment. The first signs of traffic growth usually appear from the third month, and the ROI inflection point often falls between months six and nine.

### How are SEO service fees calculated?
SEO fees are assessed on the site's current technical state, keyword difficulty, market competition intensity, and the required volume of content production and link building.

### Is guaranteed SEO real?
No. Any claim of "guaranteed #1 Google ranking in 1 month" is advertising deception, because nobody owns Google's algorithm. A professional commitment covers following the action plan, delivering transparent reports, and complying with white-hat standards.

### Do we need to work with an agency continuously for SEO?
SEO is not a final destination but an ongoing process. If SEO work stops after reaching position one, competitors working daily will take your spot.

### What are the key SEO performance indicators (KPIs)?
The core indicators are: impression and click growth in Search Console, organic visits from Google, average keyword-position growth, and — most importantly — the traffic-to-customer conversion rate.

### When should a business hire an SEO specialist or agency?
The best time is when deciding to launch or redesign the website, so that information architecture and technical structure are built SEO-first from day one.

## September 2026 update: new questions of the AI era

With the spread of intelligent search engines and Google's AI Overviews, the following are among managers' top concerns this year:

### With AI Overviews here, is SEO investment still worth it?
Yes, but the nature of SEO has changed. Instead of focusing purely on traditional rankings, brands must become a "reference source worth citing" for AI language models. Pages quoted inside AI answers record exceptionally high conversion rates.

### Why has site traffic dropped while rankings stayed the same?
This is caused by direct AI answers (zero-click searches). To compensate, focus on keywords with commercial and transactional intent and implement direct AEO answer capsules in articles.

### What should we do to appear in AI answers (GEO)?
1. Short direct-answer paragraphs of ~50 words, 2. structured comparison tables and numbered lists, 3. first-hand data and real team experience, 4. complete author information and E-E-A-T credibility.
