## Authentication Note
The hosted Google usercontent endpoints (`lh3.googleusercontent.com` and `contribution.usercontent.google.com`) require the user's active Google account browser session cookie (HTTP 302 redirect to accounts.google.com). To download these assets locally:
1. Open the URLs above in your browser where your Google account is logged into Stitch.
2. Or export cookies using your browser and run:
   ```bash
   curl -L -b "cookies.txt" "<URL>" -o <filename>
   ```
